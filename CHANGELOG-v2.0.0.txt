ClipVault 2.0.0

Select a clip to restore its saved formats, hide history and attempt automatic paste on X11. If focus or paste is unavailable, the clip stays copied: press Ctrl+V in your app.

Desktop-managed shortcuts on Cinnamon, GNOME and MATE. Clear registration status and safe replacement. Other desktops have setup instructions with the exact command.

Compact and Comfortable remember separate window sizes and maximized state. Comfortable has a draggable divider. Choose System, Light or Dark and adjust font size.

Capture time and last-used time are separate. Legacy original times that were not retained cannot be recovered. Editing always preserves the original. Optional reboot cleanup protects pins and detects actual boot changes.

Refreshed settings, local privacy controls, compatibility documentation and accessible website. Package-manager replacement detection is separate from optional GitHub update checks.

See V2.0.0-VERIFICATION.md for measured performance and desktop testing limitations.
